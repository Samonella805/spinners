Steer with W A S D, or drag anywhere on a phone. In 2 Players, player 2 uses ← ↑ ↓ → (on one phone, each player drags on their own half). A gamepad works too: left stick or D-pad, Start to pause.

Slam into your rival. Harder impacts do more damage. Hits chip parts off, and a spinner missing parts is weaker. Drain their health to shatter them. If nobody takes damage for 5 seconds, every spinner loses 5 HP. Pick an arena: its gutters, obstacles and hazards affect both spinners the same way. Press P to pause, M to mute.

Gauntlet: beat four CPU rivals in a row across four arenas. Damage carries over, with a partial repair between rounds.
