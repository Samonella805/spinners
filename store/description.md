# Spin Arena: store listing

## Title
Spin Arena

## Short description (one line, under 100 characters)
Pick a battle spinner, slam into your rival and shatter them before they shatter you.

## Long description
Spin Arena is a fast top-down battle-spinner brawler. Pick one of eight spinners, each with its own silly personality and signature trick, then ram your rival until their armour plates fly off and they shatter.

Every hit matters: harder impacts do more damage, and every plate you knock off leaves them weaker. Rounds last about a minute, so it's always "one more match".

**Eight spinners, eight ways to win**
- **Big Boy**: slow and grumpy. The longer he goes without a hit, the harder he spins.
- **Whirl**: tiny and slippery. Her gravity field drags you in and burns.
- **Clockwork**: winds up tick by tick, then slows time for everyone else.
- **Scrap-King**: magnets loose scrap into a junk shield, then throws the whole pile.
- **Eater**: heals with every bite and turns floor scrap into acid traps.
- **Monster**: every wall bounce charges a hit that lands like a truck.
- **Mama**: sends two babies to pester you. Every hug they bring home makes her stronger.
- **Paradox**: two counter-rotating rings that can catch your hit and throw it back.

**Five arenas**: Classic, Gutterworks, Crossroads, Foundry and Salvage Yard. Each has its own gutters, obstacles and hazards.

**Three ways to play**
- **Play**: you against the CPU.
- **2 Players**: on one keyboard, or two players on one phone.
- **Watch**: sit back and let two CPUs fight it out.

## Controls
- **Keyboard:** W A S D to steer. In 2 Players, player 2 uses the arrow keys. P pauses, M mutes.
- **Touch:** drag anywhere to steer. In 2 Players on one phone, each player drags on their own half.
